Family Display Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

1. Introduction

Family Display is a personal household dashboard designed to display family schedules, calendar events, tasks, grocery lists, weather information, and family photographs.

2. Google Calendar Access

Family Display uses Google OAuth authentication to access Google Calendar information with the user's authorization.

The application requests read-only calendar access to retrieve and display calendar events. It does not create, modify, or delete Google Calendar events.

3. Information Accessed

Depending on the calendars authorized by the user, Family Display may access calendar event titles, descriptions, dates, times, and related event details.

4. How Information Is Used

Google Calendar information is used to display household schedules and upcoming events on the Family Display dashboard.

5. Data Storage and Security

Family Display is designed to operate as a privately managed household application. Google authorization credentials and calendar information must be protected against unauthorized access.

6. Information Sharing

Family Display is intended for personal household use. Calendar information is displayed to authorized household users.

7. Revoking Access

Users can revoke Family Display's access to their Google account through their Google Account security settings.

Visit Google Account Connections to manage connected applications.

8. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated as Family Display evolves.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact: Family Display Administrator
