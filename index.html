About Family Display

Family Display is a personal home dashboard designed to help families stay organized and connected.

It brings together calendars, daily schedules, tasks, grocery lists, weather information, and family photos into one convenient display.

Features

  • Google Calendar integration
  • Today and Tomorrow schedules
  • Family tasks and reminders
  • Grocery lists
  • Weather information
  • Family photo slideshow
  • Responsive landscape and portrait layouts

Some features are under development.

Google Calendar Integration

Family Display uses Google Calendar authorization to display calendar events for authorized users.

Calendar access is read-only. Family Display does not modify or delete Google Calendar events.
